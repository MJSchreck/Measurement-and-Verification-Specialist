## Contract Overview & Scope

- **Project:** GSA/PBS Nationwide Deep Energy Retrofit (NDER1) – Chicago, Illinois. Largest-scope order under DOE ESPC IDIQ DE-AM36-09GO29039, TO:001. Original order GS-P-05-14-GA-0004; converted/renumbered to 47PF0023F0723.
- **ECMs (per PY10 CPARS Comments doc):** twelve ECMs among seven federal buildings — boiler plant improvement, replacement of district chilled water and chiller plant improvements, replacement/expansion of controls systems, interior/garage lighting retrofits and controls, AHU upgrades, and HVAC upgrades. NORESCO is responsible for all energy savings and for maintenance of a portion of the chiller plant equipment (JCI is its controls/chiller subcontractor; JLL is the O&M provider).
- **Known ECM specifics:** ECM-01 lighting at 536 S. Clark; ECM-9 HVAC/controls at 536 S. Clark (2016 scope — AHU SAT/static-pressure resets, mixed-air dampers, heat-recovery optimization, chilled-water plant/cooling-tower controls per Jerrud Parker's 8/3/26 email); ECM-07 chiller plant (savings calc uses a Year-5 chiller performance reference — a recurring GSA comment); ECM-13 Metcalfe BAS (appendix check assigned 8/5/26).
- **M&V cadence:** Quarterly Verification Reports (QVRs) plus an Annual Verification Report (AVR) due ~8/1 after each 6/30 performance-year end (precedent: PY7 report due 8/1/2023, submitted 7/31/2023). Savings are "essentially stipulated at this point" (Jerrud Parker, 7/31/26). Monthly WO reports, PM calendars, and 3-month look-aheads are standing deliverables; monthly GSA/NORESCO/JLL partnering meetings and quarterly site project check-ins are the standing meeting battle rhythm.
