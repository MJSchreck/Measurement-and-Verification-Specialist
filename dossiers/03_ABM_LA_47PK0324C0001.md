## Contract Overview & Scope

- Dual-phase (in practice three-tranche) ESPC across five Los Angeles federal buildings. Phase 1 SF1449 executed 12/13/2013 (GS-09P-14-KS-C-0003); Phase 2B SOW added July 2018 (GS-09P-12-KS-C-0023 reference in M0002 MFR); task-order phase GS-09-P-16-KS-D-7146; consolidated/migrated into standalone contract 47PK0324C0001 (award date 6/4/2024 per baseline; "EASi stuff for a migrated contract" session with Heidi 9/17/2025).
- **Mod PS92 (executed 4/22/2022** on GS-09P-14-KS-C-0003) consolidated Phase 1, 2A, and 2B financial schedules into a single instrument — consolidated TO-3, IGA Schedule-3, and the Hannon Armstrong Financing Termination Liability Schedule; language states the consolidated schedules "replace and supersede all prior guaranteed savings, financial schedules and payment schedules." This consolidation is the crux of the later dispute (ABM: single guarantee obligation, no standalone phases; GSA: separate schedules/interests persist and consolidation masks the Phase 2B shortfall).
- Phase interest rates: Phase 1 – 5.51%, Phase 2A – 4.33%, Phase 2B – 4.36% (IGA Summary Schedule notes 7–9, quoted in M0002 MFR).
- **Phase 2B scope:** Battery Energy Storage Systems at Glenn Anderson FB Long Beach (ECM 14.01b) and Ronald Reagan Courthouse (ECM 14.02), plus ECM 14.01a (elimination of legacy JCI ESPC / cogeneration at Anderson). BESS implementation $9,989,601 with $868,187/yr IGA savings (~96% of Phase 2B estimated savings); non-BESS Phase 2B remainder ~$2.0M implementation for only ~$36K/yr savings (~55-yr payback) (Matt's 4/30/2026 pivot memo). Phase 2B O&M savings stream ~$380K/yr, of which ~$252K/yr is JCI-buyout-avoidance (IGA Schedule 4 Note 5) and ~$128K/yr is cogen-related O&M/M&V.
- **The defining event:** the Anderson BESS caught fire **4/24/2023**; the 30-day proof-of-performance period was stopped that day and the batteries are inoperable (Year 5 Consolidated M&V Report §1.1.3). The Reagan BESS never achieved full functionality. The Phase 2B PIR was never accepted (Felipe confirmed 4/30/2026 with Mod PS85 language: PIR deferred pending 30-day test — "the contract accepts nothing regarding Phase 2B PIR"). Phase 2B conditional acceptance (7/1/2021 conditional; 1/31/2023 referenced as conditional acceptance that never converted) never became full acceptance. Batteries physically removed from both sites by 7/3/2025; ABM claims ~$500K disposal cost and no salvage value (6/30/2026 call).
- Predecessor-contract mod history in Drive (GS09P14KSC0003 series): PS04/PS05/PS06 (schedules, bond language), PS13 (O&M Option Yr 1), PA16/PA17, PS22 (Phase 2 with attachments), PS23 (Phase 1 acceptance), PS24 (Ph 2 rate lock), PA28 (accounting reconciliation), PA29 (assignment of payments), PO30 (O&M Option 2), PC34 (restore Roybal elevator O&M), PS35 (LB cooling tower repairs), PS85/PS91 (2% penalty provisions), PS92 (consolidation).
