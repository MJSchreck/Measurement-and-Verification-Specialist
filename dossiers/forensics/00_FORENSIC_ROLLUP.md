1. **Where real measurement exists, contractors miss; where stipulation rules, nobody can miss.** The only contracts showing shortfall years (Honeywell LA, ABM R8 recomputed, Battle Creek PY2, Honeywell SF Y6) are the ones where someone actually measured.
