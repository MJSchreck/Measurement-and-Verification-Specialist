## 0. Headline

This contract has **never reported an annual savings shortfall**: verified > guaranteed in every year for which a report figure could be extracted (Y1–Y7). The forensic story is not a shortfall — it is **how the surplus is manufactured and maintained**:

1. **Verified quantities have been frozen at the Year-5 level since PY5.** Years 6–11 reports re-book the Y5 verified physical quantities and only escalate dollar rates (McKinstry's own cover memo and Y11 transmittal say so explicitly). No annual re-measurement of most ECMs has occurred since ~2019 — the same period in which no government witnessing has occurred (none since PY4).
2. The **Year 5 comment cycle (2019–2024, 110 GSA/EMP2 comments, OIG A180017-driven)** clawed the Y5 claim down from **$704,882 → $667,398** (−$37,484) and the originally-issued Y6 claim from **$724,446 → $685,126** (−$39,320). That is the measure of how much over-claim the original reporting contained when someone actually checked.
3. McKinstry's reports document **~$75,165/yr of GSA-side operational erosion** (setpoint overrides, schedule changes, disabled sequences) but, per the report's own methodology, "verified savings calculations are not updated to reflect these changes" — GSA pays on savings it "will not … realize in actual."
