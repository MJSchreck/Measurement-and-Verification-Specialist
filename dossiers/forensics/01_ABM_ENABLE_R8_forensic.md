**CONTRACTOR PERFORMANCE DEFICIENCY — HIGH confidence** (as an M&V/reporting deficiency, distinct from the underlying equipment-failure causation in 3.2).

### 3.2 ECM 1.1 underlying boiler failure — who broke the boilers (drives whether *any* Arraj savings are payable 2023-present)
**DISPUTED / UNDETERMINED — pending CBCA 8937 — but the government's evidence currently supports CONTRACTOR causation at MEDIUM-HIGH confidence.** Evidence map (Gmail 19ae591a1834f11f — full CO/ABM-counsel exchange Jun–Sep 2025; COR comments Gmail thread 1864483499439641657 via Drive PDF 1rhA1RZ7Zs8F01awVWIPQfJKk9VfXNepP):

*Government-causation case (ABM / Vinson & Elkins / expert David Daniels):*
- Daniels report: heat exchangers overheated; deposits "primarily carbon" from **degradation of propylene glycol**; degradation caused by **air entrainment**; air/low fluid level = GSA maintenance failure of the heating loop; clogged side-stream filter cited as proof of GSA neglect; March–April 2022 air-in-system episode cited as proof GSA didn't monitor levels.
- ABM position: 500 ppm TDS limit applies to *make-up water only*, not the glycol/inhibitor mixture; design was "patently apparent" and accepted by GSA.

*Contractor-causation case (CO Felipe Jolles, with COR support):*
- **Warranty-void glycol:** ABM filled the system with INTERCOOL P-300 (TDS 1,500–2,000 ppm) against the RBI/Mestek IOM requirement "dissolved solids must be less than 500 ppm… Failure to follow these guidelines will void the warranty" (IOM p.50/52). Manufacturer letters (M. Abrahamson, Mestek/RBI, 8/14/2025) confirm the 500 ppm limit applies to the **system** medium, recommend Dowtherm/Dowfrost with anti-foam agents — ABM used neither.
- **ABM's own signed FPT** records TDS of **3,286 ppm** (p.106/158) and **1,725 ppm** (p.103/158) against the 500 ppm spec printed at pp.11 & 149 of the same document — ABM signed off on a warranty-voiding condition at commissioning.
- **No make-up water exists**: system charged and made up entirely from a glycol feeder ABM installed (Tolin email 6/3/2025) — collapsing ABM's "make-up water only" reading.
- **Pre-acceptance failure history:** first catastrophic failure **12/23/2020** (ABM cure-notice responses 1/7 & 1/25/2021), low-flow problems through the 2021–22 season, second failure 1/13/2023 — undercuts "GSA ran them into the ground."
- **Air-purge responsibility:** the 2022 air episode was remediated by **Tolin — ABM's own installer** (600 gal refill/bleed); GSA position: startup purge failure and/or undersized magnetic air/dirt separator (ABM design) and/or no-anti-foam glycol — all ABM-side.
- **Side-stream filter debunked:** the filter Daniels photographed serves the *district-steam HX*, installed **10/1/2023, after** the failure; Tolin 4/26/2021: no side-stream filter was ever in ABM's design or the record set.
- **Conductivity spike contemporaneous with ABM's 3/31/2021 acceptance** — supports pre-acceptance contamination (COR comment memo, 5/6/2026).
- **Jumper wire** bypassing boiler safety controls: found, origin unknown; GSA denies directing it; ABM attributes to GSA O&M — genuinely unresolved sub-issue.
- **Maintenance:** GSA states it followed the IOM maintenance requirements ABM provided; CO's challenge to ABM to cite one maintenance requirement GSA missed went unanswered in the record read.
- Contractual hook: **TO §C.5.1.a Design Warranty** (CO's stated basis for refusing to pay for faulty boilers and lighting; Section H title-transfer clause expressly "shall not relieve the Contractor's responsibility for ECM performance" — Gmail 19f612644b724f3a, 7/16/2026).
