## 2. How Much of the 14-Year "Perfect" Record Is Actually Verifiable?

This is the dossier's central forensic target. Answer: **almost none of it is independently verified; nearly all of it is contractually stipulated arithmetic.**

**(a) The "verified" numbers are deterministic.** Per §1a, every ECM's reported savings moves at exactly +3.9% (electric, contract escalator 3.919%) or +4.2% (water/O&M) every single year. The Y15 report itself confirms the mechanism: savings are "carried on the Year 1 verified basis" because collected BAS trend data "is insufficient, per M&V Plan" (Y15 report §3.2.7, challenged in PY15 Comment 16). Once Year 1 closed, every subsequent "verified" figure was computable in advance to the dollar. The +6.1% annual surplus is simply the Year 1 verified-vs-guarantee margin, escalated in lockstep — the surplus never varied because nothing was ever re-measured. (The single deviation, Y11's +7.5%, is an unexplained internal inconsistency, not a measurement — §4.3.)

**(b) ~60% of every year's claim is O&M savings that GSA's own reviewer disbelieves.** David Frank (EMP2), email 8/22/2025 (Gmail thread 198d1e868f8f11f4), verbatim: *"We have been achieving guaranteed savings each year, most of the savings are either Option A or O&M savings. **We do have some concerns about the O&M savings, since we don't believe they are actually being achieved, but agreed as a team to let this one ride out since the term is nearly complete.**"* At the Y14 composition (~$774K/yr, ~60.5%), the cumulative O&M block over 14 years is roughly **$8.4–8.5M of the $13.99M "verified" total**. The entire 14-year surplus (+$818,415) equals only ~9.7% of that O&M block: **if the never-verified O&M savings were overstated by even ten percent, the "perfect" record inverts into a cumulative shortfall.** O&M verification consisted of "verbal confirmation from PJKK staff" with no PM logs — flagged in Y14 (accepted with a "follow up next year" that never landed) and again in PY15 Comment 11.

**(c) Government witnessing: zero for Years 1–9.** Closeout Plan §5 (full drive crawl 7/27–28/2026): witnessing forms Y1–9 — "zero trace across all drives and shares." Earliest witnessing documentation is the **PY13 verification forms signed by Delaney Crain 7/31/2024 — a JCI employee, not a government witness**. PY14 has four signed forms (8/12/2025). PY15 has exactly one form with signatures (ECM 3.1: JCI Performance Engineer + PJKK PM, 4/22/2026) and **blank agency witnessing blocks on the other three** (Comment 3). So across 15 years, government-witnessed data collection is documented for at most the last two to three years, partially.

**(d) Even the operational-verification layer waves through dead equipment.** The annual reports' only real function under this architecture is to confirm the ECMs still operate. Yet: garage CO sensors non-operational/uncalibrated since Y13; PC-C-4 off since at least Y13; garage EF-2 non-operational since Y13; Fan Room C fixtures at 0% output (Y15); one sink and one toilet out (Y15) — and in every case savings were carried at the full Year 1 verified value ("JCI assumes the proposed schedule to calculate savings for non-operational equipment" — JCI response, Y13 comment sheet).

**(e) Review-era acceptance instruments are also thin.** PY10: no acceptance letter located. Y11–13: CO letters. First bilateral acceptance mod is PS0003 (Y14, 5/21/2026). PMO packages PY10–13: none submitted. Instructional Memos exist only for PY10 and PY14. The Closeout Plan's CPARS section records that GSA's own PY10 checklist (2021) documents the same deficiency pattern as PY15: "no calculation workbooks provided, screenshots submitted in lieu of trend data, sampling-plan lapses, and government-impact quantification withheld until requested."
