## Contract Overview & Scope

~20-year ESPC at the Hart-Dole-Inouye Federal Center under the DOE NDER1 IDIQ. Trane financed the capital investment and assigned the debt-service payment stream to Hannie Mae LLC in October 2013; the O&M/Performance-Period-Expense stream stayed with Trane (confirmed in the 8/14/2026 buyout verification — Annex I shows the streams separately). The controlling schedules are the **PS07 TO Schedules set** (Drive file dated 1/27/2015; the copy in the legacy library is marked "Z - USE THIS ONE" per the 8/4/2026 master inventory), backed by the locked Appendix O final-proposal workbook (9/24/2013).

ECMs referenced in the record (full ECM list resides in the IGA, not re-read here): ECM 1.1 boilers (efficiency/tolerance issue), ECM 1.2 heating hot water pumping (HHWP-2A motor power), ECM 3.3 snow melt system (vs. AHU valve allocation), chilled-water plant (CHLR-1/PFHX-1 flow meters, CHW pumping), lighting, water, and tunnel-door ECMs (cited by Trane 8/21/2026 for closeout inspection scoping), with several ECMs monitored under **IPMVP Option B** (EMCS trend data).

Key schedule values (verified to the penny in the 8/14/2026 MFR):
- **TO-3 debt service amortization:** Year 1 $496,566.72; Year 2 $507,194.60; and so forth.
- **TO-3 Performance Period Expenses:** Year 1 $40,689.00; Year 2 $41,543.47; … **GY12 $51,139.88** (mgmt/admin $2,053.19; semi-annual O&M inspections/reporting $18,478.68; M&V services $17,965.38; annual training $3,695.73; plus 15.75% indirect and 8.0% profit).
