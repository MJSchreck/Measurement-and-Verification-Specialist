## Contract Overview & Scope

UESC task order under the SDG&E Areawide Contract for energy conservation measures at three federal border/courthouse facilities in the San Diego sector. ECMs (per acceptance letter Schedule A and Cx reports on Drive): site lighting (Clear Blue Energy), HVAC — chiller upgrades ECM 8 at Otay Mesa Customhouse, boiler upgrades ECM 10/11 at Otay POE (McClure Mechanical), and photovoltaic arrays at Tecate, Otay Mesa, Otay Customhouse, and Amistad (Baker Electric). Beneficial-use dates ran 5/17/2023 – 5/3/2024.

Structure: construction period plus 12-year performance payback (5/1/2023–5/1/2035). Government pays a fixed TO-1 Schedule 5 annual payment (principal + interest + M&V) each May 1; IES prepares an annual Performance Assurance (PA) report comparing Verified Savings against a Proposed Savings benchmark escalated at 2.54%/yr. Original Year-1 benchmark $557,519; the Post-Installation Report V03 review reduced it to **$554,205** (five scope corrections totaling $9,639/yr equivalent: Customhouse OCM-1, Tecate ECM-5, Customhouse ECM-3, Tecate ECM-9A, Customhouse ECM-8 chiller). Corrected 13-year Proposed Savings total: **$8,411,716** vs. original ~$8,462,020.

Post-Installation PA Report dated 8/5/2024 (Drive); SDG&E requested final acceptance 8/29/2024, signed by CO Sarah Connor **9/3/2024** — noting "a slight savings shortfall, with the report accepted by GSA."
