## Contract Overview & Scope

Task order awarded to Honeywell under the DOE FEMP NDER2 ESPC IDIQ (DE-AM36-09GO29035) in May 2016 covering an eight-building Los Angeles Service Center portfolio (seven active after the 2022 Holifield removal). ECMs per performance-year reports and witnessing records include: chilled water system upgrades (2.1/2.2), EMCS (3.1), HVAC (4.1), lighting upgrades (5.1), building envelope (6.1), pump/valve conversions (8.1), irrigation/xeriscape (13.x), transformers (12.1), and others through ECM 17.1. M&V follows the TO M&V Plan (IPMVP-based, with sampling/rotation methodology confirmed adequate at the 7/16/2026 Y8 close-out).

Financial architecture (from PA35 MDD, TO-3/TO-5A schedules dated 7/8/2022, and the 8/13/2026 COR buyout briefing):
- Annual payment = principal + interest (advance, 3/1) + prior-year M&V (arrears after report acceptance). Year 9: P $619,300.38 + I $394,767.21 + Year 8 M&V $98,849.48 = **$1,112,917.07** obligated.
- TO-5A ledger history: **12/4/2017 ~$11.6M** and **1/2/2020 ~$3.0M** balance reductions were Honeywell returns of financed cash for descoped ECMs; the 2022 Holifield removal (AS26) was a **restructure, not a proportional principal payoff** — restructured Holifield-era financing amortizes through the payment tail (TO-1 Years 21–22 reduction). Building-level principal shares therefore cannot be cleanly derived from the current balance.
- TO §H.9 (Protection of Financier's Interest): early-cancellation buyout with a non-negotiable **2.5% termination premium**.
