## Contract Overview & Scope

UESC task order awarded 5/19/2017 to PG&E under Areawide Contract GS-00P-14-BSD-1137, covering the San Francisco Appraisers Building (630 Sansome) and U.S. Custom House (555 Battery). ECMs (per the 8/13/2026 witnessing correspondence and TO extracts on Drive): LED lighting with Encelium (TEL) lighting controls in both buildings, chiller and chilled-water pumps, lab fume hood controls, building automation system, steam isolation valves, and water fixtures. ABM implemented and performs M&V as PG&E's subcontractor; Dominion Federal provided third-party financing with payments assigned (Assignment of Claims, 2018 mod signed by then-CO Gregory Rains, countersigned by a PG&E VP) to MassMutual Asset Finance (BofA account; Barings services).

Key architecture points (from the 8/14/2026 MFR and executed TO):
- **TO Section 3:** contractor provides annual performance assurance (PA) reporting and on-site inspections for **five (5) years following acceptance** (6/30/2020).
- **TO Section 17:** contractor performance obligations cease at acceptance except Section 3 and warranties; Government payment obligation continues through the term (to 12/1/2036).
- **PA Plan provides performance assurance, not a savings guarantee** — unrealized energy/water savings are not a basis to withhold payment.
- All ECMs verified under **IPMVP Option A with stipulated values**; the Year 5 report states no physical measurements were taken for any ECM.
- **PS11 (July 2020):** revised payment/termination schedule; $1,000,000 On-Bill Financing prepayment; financed principal reduced $4,060,530 → $3,742,652 at 3.97%.
