## Contract Overview & Scope

Task order GS-P-05-17-GB-0001 was awarded 10/28/2016 to Ameresco under DOE ESPC IDIQ DE-AM36-09GO29029 for ESPC services at the Harold Washington Social Security Center, Chicago (GSA Great Lakes Region, SSA-delegated building). Construction ran 10/28/2016–10/31/2018; the savings payback (performance) period is nine years, 11/1/2018–10/31/2027 (Source: File Memo PO21; PS22 SF30).

**ECMs (per PY7 M&V Report):**
- **ECM-1: Plant Upgrades** — new central heating/cooling plant: condensing natural-gas boilers (fuel switch from steam), variable-speed magnetic-bearing centrifugal chillers, advanced chiller staging, waterside economizer, elimination of glycol chilled water. Sub-measures 1A–1D.
- **ECM-2: HVAC Optimization** — AHU replacements, high-efficiency equipment and VFDs (2A).
- **ECM-3: BAS Upgrade & Optimization** — JCI Metasys control strategies: start/stop optimization, temperature reset schedules, minimized OA flows, airside economizers (3A–3D).

Project economics (PY7 report): reduces HWSSC source energy 36% / site energy 14%; >$3.38M savings over the 9-year performance period; PY7 GHG savings equivalent to removing 1,284 passenger vehicles (annual GHG reduction 5,943 MTCDE). M&V methodology uses eQUEST simulation plus measured verification per the M&V Plan.

**Financial architecture (PS22):** Total award $17,727,105.68; implementation-period payment $14,467,683.87; financed amount $2,603,701.00; interest expense $279,420.00; post-acceptance performance-period expense $202,457.00 (per PS06 corrections). Escalation: 1.2% electric / 1.8% gas / 3.0% water.
