## Contract Overview & Scope

Honeywell and GSA Great Lakes Region (R5) entered FSS contract 47QSWA18D0057, TO 47PF0020F0671, for an ESPC ENABLE covering three federal buildings in Detroit, based on the Final IGA submitted 7/13/2020; effective 7/15/2020 (PY4 Annual Report, §1.1).

**ECMs (all complete, accepted by GSA Detroit; Full Project Acceptance 10/26/2022):**
- **ECM 1.1 — Levin Boiler Installations:** three new gas-fired condensing HW boilers replacing district steam (installed 11/8/2021; Cx report accepted 12/14/2021; ECM completion 5/24/2022). As-built: existing HW pumps/VFDs not replaced (+$1,385/yr PY4 projected).
- **ECM 1.2 — PVM Boiler Installations:** steam-to-gas boiler conversion at McNamara. As-built: DHW heaters installed electric, not gas (−$8,584/yr PY4 projected).
- **ECM 2.1 — Levin Corridor Lighting:** fluorescent → LED; additional fixtures on several floors + toggle overrides (+$3,220/yr).
- **ECM 2.2 — 985 Parking Garage Lighting:** metal halide → LED; added stairwell/mech-room fixtures; elevator lighting descoped (+$1,446/yr).

The Government is responsible for all O&M, repairs, and replacement on the ECMs. Energy rates escalate per Table 6 (electric 1.55%/yr, gas & steam 3.41%/yr, water 2.00%/yr; PY4 steam rate $36.682/MMBtu). Savings are dominated by purchased-steam elimination (~$1.6M/yr) net of gas/electric penalties.

**Financial architecture (per Mods PS0014–PS0017 financial summaries):** Implementation cost $18,548,221 + construction interest $940,201 − implementation-period payments $750,369 = financed amount **$18,738,053**; post-construction interest $4,826,481; performance-period expenses $355,817; total contract value **$24,670,720**. Year-0 payments included IGA $177,943, AFFECT Grant $230,419, and construction-period savings $342,007 (Schedule #1 note). DTE Energy Efficiency rebates were pursued at award-time (Project IDs DTE-20-72102/-72103, Sept 2020 email record).
