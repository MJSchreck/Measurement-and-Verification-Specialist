Task Order GSP0908KS0044 was awarded 9/25/2008 to Johnson Controls Government Systems, LLC under DOE ESPC IDIQ DE-AM36-97EE73568 for ESPC services at the PJKK Federal Building/Courthouse in Honolulu and the 300 North Los Angeles building. Contract escalators: 3.919% electric, 4.2% water/O&M. PY15 includes a full year of Management/Administration & M&V services funded in advance and invoiced monthly ($5,117.17/month, months 1–11; $5,117.22 month 12). The contract migrated across systems (Comprizon GS-09-08-KS-0044 → GS-P-09-16-KS-7102 → 47PK0223F0041), and mods 1–10 and 12–16 were never transferred — a documented legacy record gap.

The contract transferred from Region 9 to the Central Office Energy Division in August 2025 after the R9 RIF eliminated the region's Climate & Sustainability Branch; there is no regional COR. EMP2 (David Frank, David Berezovskiy) provides performance-period engineering review support.
