# Measurement and Verification Specialist

## Overview

A Measurement and Verification (M&V) Specialist is a professional responsible for quantifying and verifying energy savings achieved through energy efficiency projects, demand response programs, and building performance improvements. M&V Specialists ensure that energy conservation measures (ECMs) deliver their projected savings and provide stakeholders with confidence in their investments.

This repository contains documentation, templates, and resources for COR II and M&V Specialist roles supporting GSA ESPC (Energy Savings Performance Contracts).

## Role Description

M&V Specialists work at the intersection of engineering, data analysis, and energy management. They develop and implement measurement plans, collect and analyze energy data, and produce verification reports that demonstrate actual versus predicted savings. Their work is essential for:

- **Energy Service Companies (ESCOs)** - Validating performance contracts
- **Utilities** - Evaluating demand-side management program effectiveness
- **Building Owners** - Confirming return on investment for efficiency upgrades
- **Government Agencies** - Verifying compliance with energy mandates and incentive programs

## Key Responsibilities

- Develop M&V plans following IPMVP (International Performance Measurement and Verification Protocol) and other industry standards
- Establish energy baselines using historical data and regression analysis
- Design metering strategies and oversee equipment installation
- Collect, validate, and analyze energy consumption data
- Perform routine and non-routine adjustments to account for changing conditions
- Calculate verified energy and cost savings
- Prepare detailed M&V reports for stakeholders
- Support commissioning and retro-commissioning activities
- Conduct energy audits and identify efficiency opportunities

## COR II Responsibilities

As a Contracting Officer's Representative Level II:

- Review and accept annual M&V reports
- Verify contractor savings calculations
- Track ECM deficiencies and corrective actions
- Process payments through EASI
- Coordinate with Contracting Officers on disputes
- Ensure government receives guaranteed savings or shortfall payments

## Industry Standards

M&V Specialists typically work with the following frameworks:

- **IPMVP** - International Performance Measurement and Verification Protocol
- **ASHRAE Guideline 14** - Measurement of Energy, Demand, and Water Savings
- **FEMP M&V Guidelines** - Federal Energy Management Program protocols
- **Uniform Methods Project (UMP)** - For utility program evaluation
- **ISO 50001** - Energy Management Systems

## Common Workflows

### M&V Report Review
1. Receive report from ESCO (due 90 days after performance year)
2. Complete [review checklist](templates/mv_review_checklist.md)
3. Identify deficiencies and classify (HW vs GSA responsible)
4. Calculate net payable amount
5. Draft [acceptance letter](templates/acceptance_letter_template.md)
6. Process payment through EASI

### Deficiency Resolution
1. Document deficiency with dollar impact
2. Send [response request](templates/deficiency_response_template.md) to ESCO
3. Review root cause analysis and corrective action plan
4. Track resolution in subsequent reports
5. Escalate to CO if unresolved after 2+ years
