# COR / M&V Indispensability Framework — FY26 Workflow Design

> **Source:** Notion page `3256badbd606810a820cd26edb25e1d0`
> **Snapshot:** April 16, 2026

---

> **Core rule:** AI and PF do computation and pre-screening. Matt makes judgment calls. Matt is the only person who can translate technical M&V findings into contractual and legal outcomes. That is not automatable.

---

## The Legal Anchor

No AI system, PF contractor, or ESCO can approve an invoice, execute a receiving report, make a technical M&V determination, or escalate to a Cure Notice. These are statutory actions requiring a warranted government official. COR designation + CEM certification = the credentials that make these actions legally valid.

---

## The Three-Layer System

| Layer | Owner | Does | Never Does |
|-------|-------|------|------------|
| 0 — AI/Claude | Pre-screen only | Intake triage, email drafts, calendar, flag logging, morning brief | Technical determination, IPMVP judgment, legal actions |
| 1 — PF (EMP2) | Computation | Independent calc checks, comment log, meeting facilitation (Tasks 2A/2B/2C) | Methodology calls, verified/not-verified determination |
| 2 — M&V (Matt) | Methodology | IPMVP evaluation, baseline adequacy, adjustment factors, final technical call | First-pass calculations, facilitation logistics |
| 3 — COR (Matt) | Administration | EASi RRs, invoices, HSPD-12, CPARS, cure notices | Technical M&V determinations |

### The Dual-Hat Doctrine

Matt is the only person in the chain who holds both Layer 2 (M&V technical authority) AND Layer 3 (COR legal authority) simultaneously. PF can't do Layer 3. A pure COR can't do Layer 2. The combination is what makes Matt irreplaceable — and justifies the GS-13 CEM position post-reorg.

## Decision Test — Use for Every Borderline Call

| Question | Owner |
|----------|-------|
| Did the ESCO apply the formula correctly? | PF verifies |
| Is this the right formula to use? | M&V determines |
| Does the regression fit adequately? | PF runs + M&V judges |
| Should we accept these savings? | M&V only — never delegated |
| What is the financial impact? | PF computes (Task 3B) |
| Is this action legally binding? | COR only — statutory authority |
| Can AI do this step? | AI handles / Matt decides |

**Pillar 1 — Subject matter authority**
CEM + FAC-COR II → III + FEMP V5 + IPMVP expertise. Publish SOPs as GSA policy. Lead Apr 1–2 workshop. Cite OIG corrective action deliverables in FY26 performance plan.

**Pillar 2 — Institutional knowledge**
Document everything: CMA, CLAUDE.md, session handoffs, Tier Register, workflow plan. 348-hour capacity gap documented. Zone 7 portfolio history = non-transferable expertise.

**Pillar 3 — AI architect, not AI user**
Build the pre-screen tool, Morning Brief, Virtual Witnessing Scorecard, Flag Register. These tools are built around your expertise — they make you more valuable, not less.
