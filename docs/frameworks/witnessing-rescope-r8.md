## Overview

The Denver Federal Center NDER6 R8 ESPC is at construction kickoff. Heidi Johnson directed that government witnessing requirements be formally verified, confirmed against current FEMP guidance, and — if the contract does not reflect current requirements — a contract modification be issued. This is a three-phase process: **Verify → Determine → Act**.

---

## PHASE 1 — VERIFY: Establish Current Requirements

### Step 1 — Pull the M&V Plan from the Awarded Task Order

- Locate the awarded TO M&V Plan (Attachment J-8 per DOE ESPC IDIQ structure)
- Specifically pull Section C.4 and any witnessing-specific subsections
- Confirm which M&V Option (A, B, C, or D) applies to each ECM
- **Owner:** Matt Schreck
- **Source:** NDER6 R8 Task Order documents in Google Drive

### Step 2 — Review Section C.4 Contract Language

The task order contains explicit witnessing requirements at three stages. Confirm each is present and clearly defined:

| Stage | Requirement | Location in C.4 |
|-------|-------------|-----------------|
| Baseline (Pre-Installation) | All onsite measurements shall be witnessed by GSA. Contractor provides witnessing forms with Final IGA. | C.4.1.B |
| Post-Installation | M&V Plan identifies which activities require witnessing. Signed forms included with Post-Installation Report. | C.4.1.C / C.4.2.C |
| Annual M&V (Performance Period) | M&V Plan identifies what will be witnessed. Witnessing forms required with each Annual M&V Report. | C.4.1.D / C.4.2.D |

- **Owner:** Matt Schreck
- **Flag if:** Any stage is missing, vague, or inconsistent with FEMP guidance

### Step 3 — Cross-Reference Against Current FEMP Guidance

Compare contract language against:

- **FEMP Guide to Government Witnessing and Review of M&V Activities (March 2014)** — three-level witnessing framework (Level 1: spot check / Level 2: standard / Level 3: best practice — all significant ECMs)
- **FEMP M&V Guidelines v4.0, Section 10** — Government Witnessing of M&V Activities
- **FEMP M&V Guidelines v5.0** — confirm if v5.0 supersedes any v4.0 requirements applicable to this TO
- **DOE ESPC IDIQ Generation** — confirm which generation governs this TO (Gen 3 or Gen 4); guidance applicable at time of award controls
- **Owner:** Matt Schreck
- **Output:** Written comparison noting any gaps between contract language and current FEMP guidance

---

## PHASE 2 — DETERMINE: Coordinate with Nathan

### Step 4 — Draft a Brief Findings Memo to Nathan Ingersoll

Prepare a concise memo (1–2 pages) covering:

1. What the current TO C.4 language says about witnessing
2. What current FEMP guidance requires
3. Whether there is a gap
4. Whether a contract modification is recommended

- **To:** Nathan Ingersoll (nathan.ingersoll@gsa.gov)
- **CC:** none at this stage
- **Owner:** Matt Schreck drafts; Nathan reviews
- **Do NOT send to Felipe until Nathan concurs**

### Step 5 — Nathan Review and Concurrence

- Nathan reviews findings memo
- Nathan confirms: (a) gap exists and mod is needed, OR (b) current language is sufficient
- If Nathan concurs a mod is needed → proceed to Phase 3
- If Nathan determines no mod needed → document his determination and close the action item
- **Owner:** Nathan Ingersoll
- **Timeline:** Target within 5 business days of memo receipt

---

## PHASE 3 — ACT: Contract Modification (If Required)

### Step 6 — Prepare Modification Package for Felipe Jolles

If Nathan concurs a mod is needed, Matt prepares the following for Felipe:

- **Statement of Work change:** Specific language to add or revise in the M&V Plan / Task Order Section C.4
- **Justification memo:** Why the modification is needed (gap between current contract language and FEMP guidance)
- **Recommended new language:** Draft replacement or supplemental C.4 witnessing language
- **Reference documents:** FEMP Witnessing Guide (March 2014), FEMP M&V Guidelines v4.0 Section 10, applicable IDIQ generation requirements
- **Owner:** Matt Schreck prepares package; Felipe Jolles issues mod
- **Note:** Matt recommends — Felipe decides. All modification authority rests with the CO.

### Step 7 — Felipe Reviews and Issues Bilateral Modification

- Felipe Jolles reviews the modification package
- Felipe coordinates with Ameresco (ESCO) for bilateral execution
- Modification issued to update witnessing requirements in the task order
- Ameresco must update the M&V Plan to reflect the revised requirements
- **Owner:** Felipe Jolles (felipe.jolles@gsa.gov)
- **CO Authority:** Only the CO can modify the contract

### Step 8 — Updated M&V Plan Submission

Following mod execution:

- Ameresco submits revised M&V Plan incorporating updated witnessing requirements
- M&V Plan must specify:
  - Which activities will be performed onsite vs. offsite
  - Recommended level of government witnessing per ECM (per FEMP Guide)
  - Schedule for witnessing activities
  - Witnessing form templates (Appendix)
- Matt reviews revised M&V Plan for compliance with C.4 and FEMP guidance
- Matt recommends acceptance to Felipe
- **Owner:** Ameresco submits; Matt reviews; Felipe accepts

### Step 9 — Designate GSA Government Witness(es)

Per FEMP guidance and C.4:

- GSA must identify individual(s) responsible for witnessing per site **prior to** measurement activities
- Delegation may be to anyone the agency judges qualified
- COR delegation letter should include designation of M&V witness
- Confirm Irene Stamatakis as site-level Requesting Official and whether she will serve as witness
- Document witness designations in writing
- **Owner:** Matt Schreck (recommends); Nathan Ingersoll (concurs); Felipe Jolles (documents in COR file)

### Step 10 — Establish Witnessing Coordination Protocol with Ameresco

Prior to any baseline measurement activities:

- Ameresco must coordinate visit schedule in writing to GSA in advance
- GSA confirms witness availability or requests one reschedule (Ameresco may proceed after one rescheduling)
- Ameresco provides draft witnessing forms for GSA review before measurement begins
- Signed witnessing forms filed in COR Tab 06 same day as activity
- Include witnessing as standing agenda item in weekly progress meetings
- **Owner:** Matt Schreck coordinates with Ameresco POC (confirm: Matt Gagnon or designated construction PM)
