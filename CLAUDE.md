### Risk Levels
- **RED (Critical):** Active PPA exposure, overdue payments, legal escalation, missing required documents
- **YELLOW (Watchlist):** Approaching deadlines, unresolved issues, vendor responsiveness concerns
- **GREEN (Active):** Routine administration, no immediate action required
