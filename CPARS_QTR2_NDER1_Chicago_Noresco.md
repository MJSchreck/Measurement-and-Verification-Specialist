- Complex M&V requirements with multiple measurement options

## Rating Scale Reference

| Rating | Definition |
|--------|------------|
| **Exceptional** | Performance meets contractual requirements and exceeds many to the Government's benefit. Few minor problems with highly effective corrective actions. |
| **Very Good** | Performance meets contractual requirements and exceeds some to the Government's benefit. Some minor problems with effective corrective actions. |
| **Satisfactory** | Performance meets contractual requirements. Some minor problems with satisfactory corrective actions. |
| **Marginal** | Performance does not meet some contractual requirements. Serious problems with marginally effective or not fully implemented corrective actions. |
| **Unsatisfactory** | Performance does not meet most contractual requirements. Serious problems with ineffective corrective actions; recovery not likely. |
