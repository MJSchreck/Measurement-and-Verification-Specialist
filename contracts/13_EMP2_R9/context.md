## Scope of Services
This is a BPA Call under Multiple Award BPA **GS10F0255L**, Task 4B "Miscellaneous Support Services," per the governing SOW ("Alternative Financing Project Facilitation, Cost Estimating and M&V Services," dated 5/17/2022). EMP2 provides the equivalent of ~2 FTE supporting the GSA Central Office ESPC/UESC PMO and Region 9 with:
- **Task 1 – ESPC Scorecard:** maintain/enhance the portfolio-wide ESPC scorecard (contract numbers, vendors, performance periods, contract values, financed rates, utility escalation factors, expiration dates, witnessing status, annual savings, ESCO shortfalls, government impacts).
- **Task 2 – UESC Scorecard:** parallel scorecard for the UESC portfolio.
- **Task 3 – Automation:** propose/implement automated data collection from annual reports (verified/guaranteed savings, witnessing docs, government impacts, shortfalls); migrated from Smartsheet to Google Sheets.
- **Task 4 – Energy Rate Escalations & Utility Rates:** analyze contractual escalation vs. real-time utility rates and impact on savings projections.
- **Task 5 – DOE Life of Contract (LOC) Support:** review quarterly DOE LOC reports, flag inconsistencies and non-responsive regions.
- **Task 6 – Refinancing & Termination Analysis:** periodic best-value analysis of existing ESPC/UESC contracts.
- **Task 7 – Policy/Guidance Reviews:** review/propose enhancements to GSA and DOE M&V policy, guidance, and tools.
- **Task 8 – EA2020 Support:** enter energy-audit/ECM/WCM data into DOE FEMP's EISA Compliance Tracking System (CTS); IRA/ES&T tracking.
- **EO 14057 Support:** strategy support for net-zero/electrification/GHG goals tied to performance contracting.
- **Task 10 – Monthly Status Report (MSR):** MSR due to COR by last Friday of each month covering activities by task, schedule/milestones, and PMO/EA2020 summaries.
- High-level independent review of annual M&V reports (ESPC) and Performance Assurance reports (UESC) against IPMVP protocols; cost estimating/LCCA support for ECMs/WCMs upon request.

Government POC on the original SOW: Shawna Ramthun (ESPC Program Specialist/COR, Office of Facilities Management). Day-to-day COR duties for the 47PK0220F0064 order were later assigned to **Matthew Schreck** (per Mar 2025 Matt/Nathan check-in notes: "your new role as COR on the EMP2 project").
