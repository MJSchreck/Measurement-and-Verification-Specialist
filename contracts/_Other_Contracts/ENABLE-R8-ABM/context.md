## Contracting Officer's Final Decision — Arraj Boilers (separate payment/warranty dispute, RESOLVED 6/8/2026)
This is a **distinct** dispute from the M&V savings-verification issue above, but directly relevant: it establishes that the CO has already formally found ABM responsible for the boiler failure on separate grounds.
- **Document:** Contracting Officer's Final Decision (COFD) under the Disputes clause, FAR 52.233-1, dated 6/8/2026 (letter file: `Contracting_Officers_Decision_2026_06_01.pdf`). Issued by Felipe Jolles to Robert Breslin, ABM Infrastructure Solutions.
- **Subject:** Closed-loop boiler system (three RBI CK6000 units, six heat exchangers), Alfred A. Arraj U.S. Courthouse.
- **Holding: ABM's claim DENIED IN ITS ENTIRETY ($374,881.59 total).** CO found ABM installed/delivered boilers charged with INTERCOOL P-300 glycol at TDS 1,500–2,000 mg/L, violating the RBI Installation & Operations Manual's 500 PPM cap (which voids the manufacturer warranty above that level) — a **design defect** under TO §C.5.1.a (Design Warranty), for which ABM bears responsibility with no time limit (unlike the 1-year FAR 52.246-19 warranty ABM invoked).
- **Specific denials:** (i) request to modify the TO to stipulate savings for the boiler ECM — DENIED, no stipulated savings for ECMs ABM rendered inoperable through its own performance failure; (ii) $68,025.42 withheld from PY4 payment — DENIED, withholding upheld; (iii) full PY5 payment ($207,306.00 withheld) — DENIED, withholding upheld; (iv) $95,463.07 REA prep costs (GSAR 552.243-71) — DENIED.
- **Government reservation:** CO reserved all rights to recover additional damages, including remediation cost and unrealized energy savings during the offline period.
- **Dispute timeline:** boiler acceptance 3/31/2021 → first catastrophic failure 12/23/2020 (conductivity readings begin exceeding limits) → second failure 1/13/2023 (system offline since) → GSA Cure Notice 3/26/2024 (TO §C.5.1.a) → ABM REA filed 4/18/2025 → RBI/Mestek confirming letter 7/16/2025 → REA converted to certified Claim ($374,881.59) 7/31/2025 → COFD issued 6/8/2026, denied in full.
- **Appeal clock:** ABM has 90 days from **date of receipt** (not letter date) to appeal to the CBCA, or 12 months to the U.S. Court of Federal Claims. **ACTION REQUIRED: log ABM's confirmed date of receipt in the contract file the moment it's known — without it GSA cannot establish appeal timeliness.**
- **Relevance to the M&V dispute:** this COFD substantiates the COR's ECM 1.1 position — the CO has already ruled, on independent evidentiary grounds (RBI IOM, H2O Engineering conductivity logs, Citrine and Acuren expert reports), that the boiler failure is a design defect ABM is responsible for. That finding should be cited directly in the pending ECM 1.1 comment letter and any responsible-party determination.
