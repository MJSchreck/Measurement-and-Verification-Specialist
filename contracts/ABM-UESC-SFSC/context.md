## Open Issues / Flags
- **Fume Hood Controls (ECM 4.2):** TEL no longer supports the product line; site O&M manually power-cycles controllers weekly. Fume hoods overridden to run 24/7 per agency-wide mandate — savings claimed comes from AHU energy, not fume hood operation itself.
- **Lighting Controls (ECM 5.1):** Encelium occupancy sensors and battery terminals repeatedly failing/unreliable.
- **Training:** PA Plan requires ongoing Yr1–5 training; not performed in Yr1/Yr2, flagged by reviewers repeatedly. Confirmed completed and documented starting Yr3 (signed training forms provided).
- **Water rate escalation error (Yr5 review):** ABM had been escalating the water rate at the electric rate (1.62%) instead of the contracted 3.00% water escalation rate. Correct Yr5 water rate should be $23.6357/kgal (not $22.394), which raises Yr5 savings from $185,515 to $186,527 and likely affects "Savings to Date" cumulative figures in prior years' tables — **needs reconciliation, may affect years 1–5 cumulative shortfall.**
- **Warranty expirations:** Fume hood controls (ECM 4.2) warranty expired Feb 2024; lighting ECMs (both buildings) expired 7/29/2024.
- Utility rate/consumption data (actual bills) was only provided by ABM in Yr1 and Yr3 — not since, limiting GSA's ability to verify actual vs. escalated rates independently.
