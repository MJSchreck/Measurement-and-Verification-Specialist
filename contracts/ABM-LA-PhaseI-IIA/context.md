- **Cure Notice in progress over Phase 2B BESS (Battery Energy Storage System, ECMs 14.01b/14.02) — the ECMs have never been formally accepted, and GSA is withholding that portion of payment.** This is the most significant open compliance issue on this contract given its $143.9M value; track cure notice resolution closely (FAR 49.402-3 process).
- **Savings-adjustment justification gaps (flagged in most recent Yr10/8/4 M&V review):** Per FEMP PIR checklist, any ECM with verified savings varying >10% from proposed requires written justification in the report. Current report has NO savings adjustments included despite: ECM NLA-1 verified savings ~$110K over proposed; ECM 2.02 (Motorized Isolation Valves) proposed $4,873 → verified $36,940 (large unexplained jump); another ECM's verified savings dropped $63K year-over-year with no explanation.
- **Reporting clarity issue:** Section 1.3 Table 6 shows "Government impact" as $0 while a net variance of ($93,808) is displayed as ESCO responsibility — GSA reviewer flagged this as confusing/possibly mislabeled.
- **Data gaps (per "Envoity issues.xlsx" trend-data log):** No trend data for CHW Plant Enable/Status (300 NLA); Exhaust fan trend data only in 15-hour intervals vs. required 15-minute; missing trend data for fan walls 14/18 (sensors S-5, S-18, S-31, S-32) and RTU AC-5 (HP building) — these gaps limit M&V confidence for the affected ECMs.
- M&V is performed via ABM's proprietary "Niagara Analytics Framework (NAF)" rather than a GSA-standard platform (e.g., SkySpark) — per the original Risk & Responsibility matrix, ABM will hand over all NAF logic to GSA at no cost if GSA elects to standardize on SkySpark, but GSA would then own setup/configuration and any baseline adjustments from the switch.
