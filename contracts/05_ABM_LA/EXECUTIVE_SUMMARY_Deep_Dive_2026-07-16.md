## 1. Bottom line

This is one contract in GSA's tracking systems but functionally three separate ESPC projects — Phase 1, Phase 2A, and Phase 2B — bundled into a single consolidated financial schedule by **Mod PS92** (executed 4/18/2022) [Executed Mod PS92, Drive `1DOCpAEfF3zGhjNMS_25p4GcJHzn07CyM`]. Phase 1 and Phase 2A are administratively current and overperforming. **Phase 2B is in crisis**: its centerpiece measure — a Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) at two buildings — has been non-operational since a fire in April 2023, was never formally accepted, and GSA is now mid-negotiation on a **full Phase 2B termination** worth roughly **$7.2M–$10.7M**, with ABM's written counter-response due imminently (end of July 2026).

## 4. ECMs by phase

**Phase 1** (chiller-plant era): NLA chiller plant retrofit and DCV; Anderson (LB) CHW pump/cooling tower VFDs; Reagan (RG) CO-controlled exhaust fan, water-side economizer, CW pump VFDs; SSA (HP) gas-fired tankless DHW heaters and occupancy-based temperature setback.

**Phase 2A** (largest ECM count): building-wide lighting retrofit and controls; electric transformer upgrades; low-flow plumbing; Dyson hand dryers; DDC retrofit; monitoring-based commissioning; chiller replacements and cooling tower isolation valves; SSA RTU replacement; NLA fan wall and cooling coil retrofit; Reagan building envelope weatherization; Roybal/Reagan motor and VFD upgrades; extensive per-building retro-commissioning (CHW reset, static pressure reset, optimal start/stop, DCV).

**Phase 2B**: Anderson boiler/HW heater replacement; NLA cooling tower equalization; several PIC valve-sensor and CHW pressure-control measures across Anderson/Roybal/NLA; SSA and Anderson lighting; Anderson window glazing, VFD replacement, and premium-efficiency motors; **and the BESS trio — ECM 14.01a (elimination of JCI ESPC/cogeneration equipment), ECM 14.01b (Anderson battery storage), ECM 14.02 (Reagan battery storage).**

## 7. The BESS crisis and the termination negotiation

**What happened:** A fire destroyed the Anderson BESS around April 18, 2023; the 30-day proof-of-performance period was halted 4/24/2023. The Reagan BESS separately never reached full functionality. Both report **zero savings, non-operational**, in the current PY11 report [ABM Master Brief, Drive `1_4n-BaUxt__C38lIOr6E7OHS3gwpYJFG2LjJ50sIMyE`; PY11 Report §1.7].

**Cure notice status — unresolved.** On 2/24/2026, GSA (CO Felipe Jolles, EMP2, and Matt Schreck) agreed to proceed with a Cure Notice under FAR 49.402-3(d); Matt drafted it that day [MFR, Drive `14fN-ALEiu1PZdhOlEjJXcJ-vFtLl1etH3HCenwth_JU`]. As of 4/30/2026, an internal brief flags: **"CRITICAL — CONFIRM WITH FELIPE: Did the cure notice from 2/24 ever go out?"** [Felipe Check-In Brief, Drive `1e2fJ3eWWxYZIYTPAsN90yg-glSNwwcTECxqylZsolpA`]. No document in this research confirms formal issuance — the track was effectively superseded by a strategy pivot before the question was resolved.

**The pivot (4/30/2026):** GSA moved from "BESS-only descope" to **full Phase 2B termination** under PS92 Schedule #5 cancellation ceilings. Rationale: a BESS-only descope would leave GSA paying ~$1M/yr in Phase 2B debt service for only ~$36K/yr in surviving non-BESS savings.

**ABM's rejected counter:** ABM offered $4.42M to descope BESS (Dec 2024); GSA rejected it in May 2025 — a $6.68M shortfall against GSA's independently-calculated $11.1M fair value, confirmed by three separate calculation methods [Felipe Check-In Brief].

**Current termination math (COR Position Memo, 6/3/2026, Drive `1z_5pSlACG1Wdy3Qe_TgZ9a043eW7wszl66RDB1PHCRg`):**
- Gross termination figure (cost basis, 81.16%): **$10,745,175.92**
- After three credits (pre-acceptance loss adjustment, equipment salvage value, retained JCI-buyout O&M savings): **Net termination value $7,174,008**

**Negotiation status:** A follow-up meeting held 6/30/2026 (Matt, Felipe Jolles, ABM's Noah Becker/Lucas Price/Joe Frankini, EMP2's Nathan Bonus/David Frank) aligned on methodology and cleared up a billing/PO-number error that had been delaying Phase 1/2A payments. **ABM committed to a written line-item response by end of July 2026 — due imminently relative to today's date.** [Meeting notes, Drive `1n6Rx1hXfFwIlhUjrj1kIHLPlgmlK865jmoxnrsRdVh4`]
