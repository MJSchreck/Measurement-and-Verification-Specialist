## Executive Summary

OIG audit findings require PBS to improve ESPC oversight, specifically:
1. M&V witnessing by independent government personnel
2. Proper authorization for contract scope changes
3. Documentation timing alignment

**Your Role:** As COR for Zone C M&V, you must ensure compliance across all contracts.

---

## Finding 001: M&V Witnessing Violations

### What OIG Found
- PBS did NOT conduct required on-site/virtual witnessing
- O&M contractor employees improperly served as government witnesses
- Witnessing form signatures did NOT align with actual M&V activities
- PBS employees signed forms but admitted proper witnessing never occurred

### Requirements

| Requirement | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| **001A** | COR must demonstrate adequate M&V oversight training |
| **001B** | COR must independently witness ALL M&V activities |
| **001C** | NO contractor employees can serve as government witnesses |

### Your Compliance Actions

For EVERY M&V activity:
- [ ] YOU must physically or virtually witness the measurement
- [ ] Sign witnessing forms ONLY on the date you actually witnessed
- [ ] Ensure NO contractor employees sign as government witnesses
- [ ] Document your presence with date/time/location
- [ ] Retain evidence of witnessing (photos, logs, sign-in sheets)

## Finding 002: Contract Modification Failures

### What OIG Found
Specific example: $71,920.47 unnecessary costs for window inserts
- Known issue Oct 2023 → Installed May 2024 → Removed Sep 2024
- No contract modification issued despite known scope change

### Requirements

| Situation | Required Action |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Scope change identified | Notify CO immediately |
| ECM modification needed | Request formal TO modification |
| Cost impact expected | Document in contract file |
| Changed conditions | Follow Gen 3 Section H.15 procedures |

### Your Compliance Actions

- [ ] Document ALL scope changes immediately
- [ ] Notify CO of any changes requiring modification
- [ ] Never authorize changes without CO approval
- [ ] Track modifications through completion

---

## Finding 003: Unauthorized Contract Changes

### What OIG Found
PBS engineer (no contracting authority) "authorized" ESCO changes
- Result: Reduction in energy savings
- Violation: No CO notification or contract modification per FAR

### Requirements

**ONLY the following can authorize contract changes:**
1. Contracting Officer (CO)
2. Contracting Officer's Representative (COR) - within delegation scope

**You CANNOT authorize:**
- Scope changes beyond delegation authority
- Financial modifications
- Contract term changes
- Performance requirement changes

### Your Compliance Actions

- [ ] Know your delegation authority limits
- [ ] Escalate ALL changes to CO
- [ ] Document every communication about potential changes
- [ ] Never verbally authorize contractor changes

## Witnessing Form Requirements

### Before Signing ANY Form

1. **Were you present?**
   - Physically at the site, OR
   - Virtually connected during measurement

2. **Is the date correct?**
   - Form date MUST match actual witnessing date
   - Never backdate or forward-date

3. **Did you verify the measurement?**
   - Observed equipment/meter readings
   - Confirmed methodology compliance
   - Documented any discrepancies

4. **Are contractor signatures appropriate?**
   - Contractor signs as contractor representative
   - NEVER as government witness

### Form Checklist

- [ ] Date matches actual witnessing date
- [ ] Your signature as COR/government witness
- [ ] Contractor signature as contractor (not government)
- [ ] Location documented
- [ ] Measurement method noted
- [ ] Any discrepancies documented
