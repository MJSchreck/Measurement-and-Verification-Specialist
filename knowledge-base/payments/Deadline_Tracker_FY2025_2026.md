## PROMPT PAYMENT ACT COMPLIANCE

Per 5 CFR 1315 (Prompt Payment):
- **Payment Due:** 30 days from receipt of proper invoice
- **Documentation Deadline:** 15 days before payment due date
- **Interest Penalty:** Automatic if payment late

### Key Milestones per Payment

| Milestone | Days Before Payment | Action Required |
|-----------|---------------------|-----------------|
| Invoice Receipt | -30 days | Log receipt, begin review |
| M&V Verification | -25 days | Verify savings calculations |
| COR Review Complete | -20 days | Complete technical review |
| Documentation Package | -15 days | Finalize payment authorization |
| CO Approval | -10 days | Submit for CO signature |
| Payment Processing | -5 days | Confirm payment initiated |
| **Payment Due** | **0** | **Prompt Payment Act deadline** |
