### CMVP Certification Details

The **Certified Measurement & Verification Professional (CMVP)** credential is the gold standard for M&V professionals.

**Requirements:**
- Bachelor's degree or equivalent experience
- Minimum 3 years of M&V-related work experience
- Pass the CMVP examination
- Agree to the Code of Ethics
- Maintain certification through continuing education

**Examination Topics:**
- IPMVP concepts and application
- Baseline development and adjustments
- Savings calculations and uncertainty
- M&V plan development
- Metering and data collection
- Statistical methods

## Experience Requirements

### Entry Level Position

| Requirement | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| Education | Bachelor's degree in engineering or related field |
| Experience | 0-2 years in energy efficiency, engineering, or related field |
| Certifications | None required; CEM or CEA candidate preferred |
| Skills | Basic Excel, building systems knowledge, analytical ability |

### Mid-Level Position

| Requirement | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| Education | Bachelor's degree required; Master's preferred |
| Experience | 3-5 years in M&V or energy efficiency consulting |
| Certifications | CMVP required; CEM preferred |
| Skills | Advanced analysis, M&V plan development, project leadership |

### Senior Position

| Requirement | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| Education | Bachelor's degree required; Master's or PE preferred |
| Experience | 7+ years with demonstrated M&V project leadership |
| Certifications | CMVP required; PE and/or additional certifications |
| Skills | Complex project design, team leadership, business development |

## Continuing Education

M&V professionals should maintain skills through:

- **CMVP Recertification**: 30 CEUs every 3 years
- **CEM Recertification**: 30 CEUs every 3 years
- **Technical webinars and workshops**
- **Industry conference attendance**
- **Peer review and collaboration**
- **Publication and presentation**

## Background Requirements

### Standard Requirements

- Valid driver's license (field work may be required)
- Ability to access mechanical spaces and rooftops
- Background check may be required for government projects
- Export control compliance for federal work

### Physical Requirements

- Ability to carry monitoring equipment (up to 25 lbs)
- Comfortable working in mechanical rooms and equipment areas
- Ability to climb ladders and access building systems
- Work may require occasional evening/weekend site visits
